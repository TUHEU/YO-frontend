Yo-B reads the grammar of the street.

A small compiler-construction pipeline — lexer, grammar transformer, and LL(1) parser — built on real taxi, market, and campus talk from Yaoundé.

1 · Collecte

Transcribed statements from taxis, chop houses, bendskins, the market, and campus — kept exactly as spoken, slang and mistakes included.

2 · Lexique

A hand-written lexer splits each sentence into nouns, verbs, slang, and code-mixed expressions using regular expressions and lookup tables.

3 · Syntaxe

A context-free grammar is built, then transformed — left recursion removed, common prefixes factored — before FIRST/FOLLOW and an LL(1) table are computed.

4 · Parseur

A predictive LL(1) parser reads the tagged token stream and decides, step by step, whether a sentence fits the grammar.
